# Contract review

## Summarize
Parties, scope, price and payment terms, term and renewal (auto-renewal?), termination rights and fees, exclusivity and minimum purchase, delivery and service levels, warranties, liability caps and indemnities, insurance requirements, personal guarantees, governing law and disputes, data and IP (for software).

## Red flags
- Auto-renewal with long notice windows.
- Exclusivity or minimum volumes we may not hit (roaster, dairy, equipment).
- Uncapped liability or one-sided indemnity.
- Personal guarantees.
- Equipment leases whose total cost exceeds buying by more than ~30% (compute it).
- Contractor contracts without fixed scope, schedule, change-order process, lien waivers and permits responsibility.
- Hurricane and force-majeure gaps.

## Output
A one-page summary table, a red/amber/green list of clauses, and suggested redlines. Add a contract to the contract register (key dates). State that a licensed Florida attorney should review material contracts before signing.
