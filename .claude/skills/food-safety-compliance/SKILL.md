# Food safety compliance (Florida DBPR)

## Rules (docs/05, docs/06)
- Food managers must pass an accredited certification exam within 30 days of employment; recertify every 5 years.
- If 3 or more food employees work at once, a certified manager must be present at all times.
- All food employees need approved food-safety training (deadline commonly cited as 60 days from hire; To verify).
- Proof of certification must be available at inspection.

## Coffee-shop hot spots
Milk and alt-milk holding temperatures (at or below 41°F cold), steam wand cleaning, ice machine and ice scoop handling, cold brew batch dating and storage, pastry display, handwashing sink access, 3-compartment sink, chemical storage, pest control, date labelling.

## Daily logs
Fridge and freezer temperatures (opening and mid-shift), sanitiser concentration, cleaning checklist, cold brew and batch labels, employee illness reporting.

## Allergens
Dairy, soy, nuts (almond and other nut milks), gluten in pastries; label in English and Spanish; cross-contact steps for alt milks.

## Inspection readiness
Monthly self-inspection using the DBPR inspection form categories; fix high-priority items immediately; keep records on site.
