# POS and tech stack

## Requirements
Fast counter and ventanita checkout; modifiers (milks, shots, sweetness); mobile ordering and pickup; loyalty and gift cards; delivery app integration; tax rates; tips; staff timeclock and scheduling; inventory by item; reporting and data export; offline mode; bilingual receipts.

## Cost benchmarks (docs/04)
POS hardware $500–$2,000 per terminal; software $50–$200/month; processing 2.5–3.5% per transaction; Wi-Fi setup $500–$1,500; cameras $500–$2,000; music licensing $500–$1,500/year.

## Evaluate
Compare 2–3 platforms (for example Square, Toast, Clover) on fit to requirements, 3-year total cost, processing rate, contract lock-in, and integrations. Run a demo with a real menu.

## Setup checklist
Menu and modifiers, tax rates (7% in Miami-Dade), tip settings, loyalty rules, gift cards, online ordering page, delivery integrations, staff roles and permissions, reports, backups, separate guest Wi-Fi network.
