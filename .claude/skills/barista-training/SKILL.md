# Barista training

## Curriculum (2–4 weeks before opening; docs/06)
1. Coffee basics: origins, our roaster, freshness, grind and extraction.
2. Espresso: dose, distribution, tamp, ratio and time, tasting and dialing in.
3. Milk: texturing for latte, cappuccino, cortado; alt milks; latte art.
4. Cuban espresso: cafecito with espumita, colada service, cortadito, café con leche.
5. Brew: batch, pour-over, cold brew production and storage.
6. Workflow at peak: station roles, sequencing, mobile orders.
7. Cleaning and food safety (with `food-safety-compliance`).
8. Hospitality in English and Spanish, upselling, service recovery.

## Certification ladder
Trainee → barista (passes practical: 10 drinks to standard in a timed set) → senior barista → shift lead. Tie pay steps to levels.

## Ongoing
Weekly 30-minute skill sessions, monthly cupping, peer coaching, drink audits against recipe cards.
