# Menu costing and pricing

## Item cost card
For each item: recipe (grams of coffee, ml of milk or alt-milk, syrup, cup, lid, sleeve, straw), unit cost of each input, waste allowance (3–5%, Assumption), total cost, price before tax, cost %, and contribution margin.

## Price ladder (Miami)
- Entry: Cuban espresso line (cafecito, colada, cortadito) around $3–4.50, anchored to local ventanitas (~$3 colada; GROU lists colada/espresso $3.29, cortado $4.39).
- Core: espresso drinks $5–7.
- Premium: signatures, pour-over, specialty iced $7–9+.
- Add-ons: alt milk, extra shot, syrups (high margin).
- Food and retail bags to lift the average ticket.

## Targets
Beverage cost 15–20%; blended COGS 25–35%; price ends consistent; sales tax (7% in Miami-Dade) shown or included consistently.

## Repricing triggers
- Green coffee or roaster price moves more than 10%.
- Any item above its cost target for a month.
- Model the effect on ticket and volume before changing prices; change several items modestly rather than one item sharply.
