# Funding packaging

## Options to compare
| Option | Notes |
|---|---|
| Owner savings / friends and family | Cheapest control-wise; document terms |
| SBA 7(a) loan | Main SBA program for startups; lender wants a plan, projections, owner equity injection, personal guarantee |
| SBA microloan (up to $50K) | Through intermediaries; smaller amounts |
| Equipment financing or leasing | Spreads espresso/refrigeration cost; compare total cost |
| Investors (equity or revenue share) | Dilution and control terms |
| Customer pre-sales / credits | Community funding; model redemption cost |
Verify current SBA terms, rates and equity-injection rules on sba.gov before stating them.

## Lender package
Executive summary, concept, market and competition (docs/01–03), management bios, startup budget and use of funds, 36-month projections with assumptions, break-even, collateral, personal financial statement list, lease LOI, permits status.

## Rules
- Total capital = startup budget + 10–15% contingency + 3–6 months working capital.
- Show debt service coverage in base and downside cases.
- Flag terms that need an attorney or CPA.
