# Brand identity

## Positioning statement
For <target segment> in <neighborhood>, <brand> is the <category> that <key benefit>, because <reason to believe>. Unlike <competitor type>, we <difference>.

## Brand story
Origin, why Miami, the bridge between Cuban coffee culture and specialty (if that concept wins), the people and sourcing.

## Voice
Three adjectives with "this, not that" examples; bilingual voice rules (with `bilingual-localization`).

## Naming checks
Easy to say in English and Spanish; no unwanted meanings; Florida Sunbiz name availability; USPTO trademark search; domain and social handles; not confusingly similar to local competitors.

## Visual brief
Mood, colors and type direction, logo uses (cup, bag, ventanita sign, app icon), photography style, signage and interior cues. Brief a designer; do not imitate competitors' identities.
