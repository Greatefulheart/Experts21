# Hiring and onboarding

## Roles and pay (docs/06, Estimates)
GM/head barista $55K–$70K; shift lead $17–$20/hr + tips; barista $15–$18/hr + tips; prep $15–$16/hr. Bilingual strongly preferred.

## Hiring process
Job post (English and Spanish) → phone screen → working interview (paid) → reference check → offer. Use a structured interview with the same questions and a scoring guide; test hospitality and teamwork, not only coffee skills.

## Onboarding (first 2 weeks)
Paperwork (I-9, E-Verify if required, payroll), handbook, food-safety training, barista training plan, shadow shifts, 30-day check-in.

## Retention
Living-wage base plus tip pool, predictable schedules published 2 weeks ahead, training ladder with pay steps, free drinks and a shift meal, recognition, small bonus pool tied to reviews or sales, exit interviews.
