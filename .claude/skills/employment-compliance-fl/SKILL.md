# Employment compliance (Florida)

## Facts (docs/06)
- Minimum wage $15.00/hr from Sept 30, 2026 (floridajobs.org); tipped cash wage $11.98 with a $3.02 tip credit; tipped employee = more than $30/month in tips.
- Private employers with 25+ employees must use E-Verify for new hires (FS 448.095); all employers complete Form I-9.
- Workers' compensation: required for non-construction employers with 4+ employees (To verify).

## Tip design checklist
- Using the tip credit requires notice to employees and that tips bring them to at least minimum wage.
- Tip pools may include baristas and other non-managers; managers and supervisors may not keep tips from the pool (FLSA). Document the pool rules.
- Card tips: decide on processing-fee handling and payout timing; keep records.

## Hiring checklist
Offer letter, I-9 within 3 business days, E-Verify if at or above 25 employees, new-hire reporting, payroll setup, posters (Florida minimum wage, federal FLSA, OSHA, EEO), food-safety training, handbook acknowledgment.

## Minors
Check Florida child labor hour limits before hiring under-18s.
