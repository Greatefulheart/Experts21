# Launch and events planning

## 8-week launch (docs/07)
| When | What |
|---|---|
| T-8 weeks | Brand, bilingual menu, Google profile, Instagram "coming soon" series |
| T-4 weeks | Email/SMS list (free drink at opening); meet neighbors and building managers |
| T-2 weeks | Soft opening for neighbors, creators, press; collect reviews |
| Opening week | Grand opening event; loyalty sign-up offer; tasting flights |
| +1–3 months | Weekly content; first B2B accounts; adjust the menu from POS data |
Budget $4K–$20K pre-opening, then 1–3% of revenue monthly (Estimates).

## Miami calendar (confirm dates each year)
Peak season Nov–Apr (aim to open Oct–Nov); Art Basel Miami Beach and Miami Art Week (early December); Miami Music Week / Ultra (March); Miami Open and F1 Miami Grand Prix (spring); National Coffee Day (Sept 29); summer slowdown June–Sept; hurricane season June 1–Nov 30.

## Event checklist
Goal and metric, partner, offer, staffing and stock, permits (outdoor events), content plan, follow-up.
