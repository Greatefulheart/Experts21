# Inventory and procurement

## Par levels
Par = (average daily usage x days between deliveries) + safety stock (1–2 days; more in hurricane season). Set for coffee, milks, cups and lids by size, pastries, syrups, cleaning supplies.

## Vendor comparison
Unit price, minimum order, delivery days and windows, payment terms, quality, reliability, local backup supplier, contract terms (use `contract-review`).

## Routines
- Daily: milk and pastry counts; waste log.
- Weekly: full count of key items, order, compare actual vs. theoretical usage (from POS item sales).
- Monthly: COGS review vs. target; vendor scorecard.

## Hurricane season (June 1–Nov 30)
Pre-storm stock of shelf-stable items and packaging; plan for spoilage of dairy; backup suppliers; insurance documentation of inventory.
