# Coffee sourcing and quality

## Market context (docs/01, docs/08)
Arabica peaked at $4.41/lb (Feb 2025) and traded ~$2.72/lb on Nov 20, 2026; USDA forecasts a record 2026/27 Brazil crop. Tariffs on Brazil were imposed in 2025 and removed for green coffee in Nov 2025. Expect volatility.

## Roaster evaluation
Shortlist local Miami roasters and 2–3 national ones. Score: cup quality (blind cupping, SCA form), consistency, price per lb at our volume, contract terms (fixed price 6–12 months), delivery frequency, training and equipment support, story and transparency, wholesale exclusivity in our area. Note local roasters may also be competitors.

## Recipes (starting points to dial in)
- Espresso: ratio ~1:2, 25–32 s; set dose and yield per blend.
- Cuban espresso: darker blend, sugar whipped into the first drops (espumita); consistent sweetness.
- Filter: ~1:16; cold brew ~1:8 concentrate, labelled and dated.

## Quality control
Daily dial-in and taste check, weekly cupping, grinder calibration log, customer-feedback loop.

## Price risk
Fixed-price contracts, menu repricing triggers (more than 10% cost change), monthly price watch.
