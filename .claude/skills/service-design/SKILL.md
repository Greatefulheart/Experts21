# Service design

## Journey map per segment
Arrive → see menu → order (counter, ventanita, app) → pay → wait → pick up → stay or leave → return. For each step: what the customer needs, pain points, our standard.

## Flow rules
- Separate mobile-order pickup from the ordering queue.
- Ventanita for fast Cuban espresso orders without entering (commuters, regulars).
- Target order-to-drink time at peak (e.g., under 4 minutes; Assumption to validate).
- Menu readable from the queue; bilingual.

## Space
Seat mix (counter, two-tops, communal), outlets and Wi-Fi for remote workers, a laptop policy for peak hours, shade and AC, restroom access, outdoor seating where permitted.

## Hospitality standards
Greeting within 10 seconds, name on cup, language preference respected, regulars recognised, a simple service-recovery rule (remake or comp, no argument).
