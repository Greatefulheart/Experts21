# Equipment specification

## Budget ranges (docs/04)
2-group espresso machine $10K–$35K (budget models from ~$5K); grinders $1.5K–$8K each; batch brewer $0.5K–$5K; water filtration $0.5K–$6K; refrigeration $1.5K–$26.5K; optional electric roaster ~$15K–$20K.

## Sizing
1. Forecast peak drinks per hour (from the financial model).
2. Groups: 2-group for most cafés; 3-group if peak is above ~150 drinks/hour (Assumption to validate with vendors).
3. Grinders: at least 2 espresso grinders (house + decaf or Cuban blend) plus a batch grinder.
4. Cold program: cold brew system, ice machine sized for Miami heat (iced drinks year-round).

## Spec sheet per item
Model options, capacity, power (voltage, amps, dedicated circuit), water in, drain, footprint, heat output, price new/used/lease, warranty, local service availability, lead time.

## Maintenance
Water hardness test and filtration; daily backflush and cleaning; quarterly gasket and screen service; annual preventive maintenance; spare parts kit; backup plan (batch brew) if the machine fails.
