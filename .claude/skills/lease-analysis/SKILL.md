# Lease analysis

## Extract these terms
Base rent and escalations; NNN/CAM estimate and caps; security deposit; term and renewal options; rent commencement; free-rent months; TI allowance; permitted use and exclusive-use clause; permit/financing contingency; personal guarantee (amount, burn-off); assignment/sublease; relocation; co-tenancy; hours; signage; HVAC responsibility; casualty, hurricane and force majeure; restoration at exit; grease trap and venting responsibility.

## Compute
- Total occupancy cost per month = base rent + NNN/CAM + any percentage rent.
- Occupancy as % of forecast revenue (target at or below 10–12%; warning above 15%).
- Total lease commitment over the term, and the guarantee exposure.

## Must-haves to negotiate (from docs/08)
- Lease contingent on permits (CU, DBPR) being issued.
- Rent starts at Certificate of Use or opening, not at signing.
- 1–3 months free rent and a TI allowance for buildout.
- Exclusive use for coffee in the building or center.
- Limited or burning-off personal guarantee; short initial term with renewals.
- Clear casualty and hurricane-closure rent abatement.

## Output
A term-sheet comparison table, red/amber/green flags, and a counter-proposal list. State that a licensed Florida attorney must review before signing.
