# Buildout project management

## Benchmarks (docs/04)
Former café $15K–$40K (2–6 weeks); converted retail $40K–$80K (8–14 weeks); raw shell $80K–$150K+ (16–24 weeks). Overruns typically 10–30%.

## Plan
1. Scope from concept, equipment list and code: plumbing (water lines, floor drains, grease trap if cooking), electrical (dedicated espresso circuits), HVAC (Miami heat load), ADA restroom and counters, finishes, signage, storm protection.
2. Get 3 bids on the same scope; level them line by line.
3. Schedule with milestones: drawings, permits, demolition, rough-in, inspections, finishes, equipment install, final inspections, CU.
4. Budget with 10–15% contingency; track committed vs. spent weekly.

## Change control
Every change: reason, cost, schedule impact, approval. Report cumulative overrun against contingency.

## Miami specifics
Hurricane-rated openings or shutters, flood elevation for equipment, backup power provisions, summer rain delays, inspection lead times.
