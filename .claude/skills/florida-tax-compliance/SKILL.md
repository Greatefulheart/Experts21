# Florida tax compliance

## Facts (docs/04, docs/05)
- Sales tax on prepared food and drinks in Miami-Dade: 6% state + 1% county surtax = **7%** (floridarevenue.com).
- File sales tax on form DR-15 at the frequency the Florida Department of Revenue assigns.
- Extra local taxes may apply by location: 2% tourist food-and-beverage surtax for hotel-licensed outlets in Miami-Dade; Miami Beach resort tax (To verify the rate and scope for our site).
- Reemployment (state unemployment) tax account required once you have employees; the E-Verify certification is reported on this return.
- Local Business Tax Receipt from Miami-Dade County plus the city BTR; renew yearly.

## Checklist
1. Register for sales tax and reemployment tax with FDOR before opening.
2. Configure POS tax rates per item type; test a receipt.
3. Set the filing calendar (sales tax, payroll deposits, quarterly reemployment, BTR renewals by Sept 30, Sunbiz annual report by May 1).
4. Track tips and tip reporting with the payroll provider.

## Rules
Rates and rules change; confirm on floridarevenue.com and with a CPA before stating them as final.
