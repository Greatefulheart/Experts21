# Site scorecard

## Gather per site
Address and municipality; listing rent ($/sq ft/yr) and NNN/CAM; size; previous use (second-generation café/restaurant?); frontage and visibility; morning-side sun and shade; parking and transit; foot-traffic counts; competitor count within 0.5 mile; FEMA flood zone (msc.fema.gov); zoning and whether food service is allowed; signage rights; outdoor seating potential.

## Score (1–5) and weights
| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Morning foot traffic / target-segment density | 25% |
| Occupancy cost as % of forecast revenue (target at or below 10–12%) | 20% |
| Buildout condition (second-generation food space scores high) | 15% |
| Competition and differentiation room | 10% |
| Access: parking, transit, walkability | 10% |
| Risk: flood zone, hurricane exposure, crime | 10% |
| Zoning and permit simplicity | 10% |

## Rules
- Benchmarks: Miami retail asking rent ~$44.5/sq ft; Little Havana ~$37; premium up to $100+ (docs/04). Add NNN ($10–$20/sq ft, Assumption) until quoted.
- Any "deal-breaker" (zoning prohibits food service, rent over 15% of revenue, ground floor in a high-risk flood zone without mitigation) overrides the score.
- Output a ranked table plus a short narrative for the top two.
