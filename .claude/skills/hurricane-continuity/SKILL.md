# Hurricane and continuity plan

Season: June 1–Nov 30. Monitor the National Hurricane Center (nhc.noaa.gov) and Miami-Dade emergency management.

## Before the season
Insurance review and documentation (photos, inventory list); shutters or impact glass tested; equipment elevated where possible; backup power plan (generator or none, and what it runs); cloud POS with offline mode; staff contact tree; supplier backup list; cash reserve for the deductible.

## Watch (36–48 hours)
Confirm staff plan; reduce perishable orders; back up data; secure outdoor furniture; fill ice; post closure plan on social and Google.

## Warning (up to 36 hours)
Close safely; shutters on; unplug and protect equipment; move inventory off the floor; photos for insurance; staff home safely.

## After the storm
Check safety, power and water; document damage; food-safety check of cold items (discard if over 41°F for 4+ hours); call insurer; communicate reopening; consider serving the community (coffee for neighbors) if safe.

## Continuity
Equipment failure → batch brew fallback; power outage → closure or limited menu; staff shortage → reduced hours.
