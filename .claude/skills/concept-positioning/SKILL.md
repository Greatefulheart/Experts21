# Concept and positioning

## Options on the table (docs/07, docs/04)
1. Cuban x specialty: ventanita window plus café; $3–4 Cuban line and specialty menu.
2. Neighborhood third place / cowork café: long stays, food, memberships.
3. Commuter grab-and-go: small footprint, mobile-order led.
Formats: kiosk ($25K–$100K), small café 500–1,000 sq ft ($100K–$200K), full café 1,000–2,000 sq ft ($150K–$300K+), café plus kitchen ($200K–$400K+), optional roaster (~$15K–$20K electric).

## Scorecard (weights are a starting point; agree them first)
| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Fit with target segment demand | 25% |
| Differentiation vs. local competitors | 20% |
| Startup cost and payback | 20% |
| Operating complexity and labor | 15% |
| Risk (seasonality, permits, hurricanes) | 10% |
| Brand and growth potential (wholesale, second site) | 10% |
Score 1–5 per criterion, show the reasoning, then sensitivity-check: does the winner change if the top two weights swap?

## Concept brief (one page)
Name placeholder, positioning statement, target segments, format and size, seats, hours, menu scope, price ladder, service model, signature experience, startup budget range, key risks, open questions.
