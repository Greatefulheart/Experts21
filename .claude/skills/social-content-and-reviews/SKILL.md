# Social content and reviews

## Why (docs/07)
Directories rank Miami coffee shops by Google rating and review count (e.g., La Colada Gourmet 3,823 reviews); 36% of away-from-home coffee buyers ordered via an app (NACS via SNS Insider).

## Content calendar
3–5 posts a week: drink-making reels, behind the bar, origin stories, team, regulars (with consent), neighborhood, events. Mix English and Spanish. Pre-opening "coming soon" series from T-8 weeks.

## Google Business Profile
Set up before opening: categories, hours, photos, menu, ordering link, attributes (Wi-Fi, outdoor seating), posts weekly.

## Review program
Ask satisfied customers at pickup and via receipts; never buy or gate reviews. Reply to every review within 48 hours in the reviewer's language; for negative reviews: thank, own it, fix offline.

## Measure
Reach, saves and shares, follower growth, profile views, direction requests, review count and rating, app-order share.
