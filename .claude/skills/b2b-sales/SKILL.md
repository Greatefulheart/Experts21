# B2B sales

## Offers
Coffee boxes (96 oz) and pastry trays for meetings; recurring office coffee service; event catering; retail bags and gift sets; wholesale beans to restaurants (if roasting or via a roaster partnership); building lobby pop-ups.

## Targets
Brickell and Downtown office towers, coworking spaces, hotels, gyms and studios, schools and universities (UM, FIU), event planners.

## Process
Prospect list → intro (bilingual) → tasting → proposal with pricing and delivery terms → trial → recurring order. Track pipeline value and conversion.

## Economics
Price each offer with `menu-costing-and-pricing`; include packaging, delivery time and labor; minimum order; payment terms (net 15/30 adds cash-flow need). Check that B2B does not slow the morning rush (schedule prep).
