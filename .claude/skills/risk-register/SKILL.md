# Risk register

## Current top risks (docs/08)
1 Undercapitalization; 2 Thin margins / low volume; 3 Lease and occupancy cost; 4 Labor cost and turnover; 5 Hurricanes, flooding, insurance; 6 Seasonality; 7 Competition and price anchoring; 8 Green coffee prices and tariffs; 9 Permit and buildout delays; 10 Equipment failure; 11 Compliance missteps.

## Register columns
ID, risk, cause, consequence, likelihood (1–5), impact (1–5), score, owner (agent or person), mitigation, early-warning trigger, contingency plan, status, last reviewed, evidence/source.

## Process
- Review when a major decision is made (site, lease, funding, concept) and monthly after opening.
- Re-score with evidence; never lower a score without saying why.
- Escalate any risk scoring 16+ to the engagement lead and client.
