> **When to use:** Use for any permit, license or zoning question, a permit timeline, or when a site or lease decision depends on what can be permitted.

# Permit pathway

## Step 1: jurisdiction
Miami-Dade has 30+ municipalities (City of Miami, Miami Beach, Coral Gables, etc.) plus unincorporated areas. Look up the address on the county property appraiser site; use that municipality's portal. docs/05 follows the City of Miami path.

## Step 2: checklist (City of Miami path, docs/05)
| Permit | Agency | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Zoning check (Miami 21) | City | Before signing the lease |
| Building permits | City/county building dept | Plumbing, electrical, mechanical, signs |
| DBPR food service license (+ plan review if required) | DBPR Hotels & Restaurants | Seating 1–49 seats $262 (verify) |
| DERM operating permit / WASD review | Miami-Dade | Some uses need DERM before the CU; grease trap if cooking |
| Certificate of Use (includes Fire Safety Permit) | City | Needs DBPR approval for food service |
| City Business Tax Receipt | City | Same portal as CU |
| County Local Business Tax Receipt | MDC Tax Collector | Cuba declaration; up to 3 business days |
| Sidewalk café permit | City Resilience & Public Works | Only for public-sidewalk seating |
| Sales and reemployment tax registration | FDOR | |

## Step 3: sequence and timeline
Zoning → entity/EIN → lease with permit contingency → drawings → building permits + DBPR plan review → buildout → inspections → CU, BTRs → tax registrations → open. Estimate 3–9 months from lease to opening.

## Output
A dated tracker: permit, agency, owner, documents, fee, submitted, status, next action. Flag anything that should change the lease or site choice. Always confirm with the agency; this is not legal advice.
