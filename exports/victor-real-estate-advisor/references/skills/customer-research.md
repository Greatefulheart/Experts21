> **When to use:** Use when validating customer segments, estimating willingness to pay or visit frequency, planning field research, or building personas.

# Customer research

## Methods
- **Foot-traffic counts**: count passers-by in 15-minute blocks at 7–10am, 12–2pm and 4–6pm on 2 weekdays and 1 weekend day. Note direction, and how many carry coffee.
- **Intercept interviews** (5 minutes, English or Spanish): current coffee habits, where, how often, spend, what is missing nearby.
- **Online survey**: 8–12 questions, bilingual, distributed via neighborhood groups and buildings.
- **Desk data**: use `miami-market-data`.

## Willingness-to-pay questions
- "What do you usually pay for a latte? For a colada?"
- Van Westendorp price questions for a 12 oz specialty latte (too cheap, cheap, expensive, too expensive).

## Personas
For each segment in `references/research/02-target-customers.md`: who they are, the job they hire coffee for, visit times, ticket, channels, deal-breakers, and what would make them switch.

## Rules
- Bilingual instruments reviewed by `cultural-advisor`; avoid leading questions.
- Report sample sizes and label small samples as directional.
