# Insurance analysis

## Benchmarks (research 08)
Florida small businesses pay $700–$2,500/yr for general liability and $900–$3,000 for a BOP (2026); Florida businesses pay 15–40% more for insurance; some South Florida property policies run $18K–$75K+/yr; hurricane deductibles are a percentage of insured value.

## Coverage checklist
General liability; property (building improvements, equipment, inventory) including named-storm wind; flood (NFIP or private; check the FEMA zone); business interruption and civil authority (closures ordered by authorities); spoilage; equipment breakdown; workers' comp; cyber (card data); employment practices; umbrella. Match lease insurance requirements.

## Compare quotes
Premium, limits, deductibles (named-storm % deductible in dollars), exclusions, waiting periods for business interruption, carrier rating. Model a worst-case hurricane: deductible + uninsured loss + closure weeks.

## Rule
Get real broker quotes per candidate address; label all figures as quotes or estimates.
