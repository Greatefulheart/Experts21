# Competitor mapping

## Map
1. Start from `references/research/03-competitors.md`.
2. List every seller within 0.5 mile (walkable areas) or 5 minutes' drive (suburbs): specialty cafés, ventanitas and Cuban counters, chains, bakeries and cafés with espresso, convenience and gas stations with coffee.
3. For each: name, address, distance, type, hours, seats, Google rating and review count, price level, signature items, roasting or not, outdoor seating.

## Mystery-shop checklist
- Price of: cafecito or colada, cortadito, espresso, 12 oz latte, oat-milk surcharge, cold brew, pastry.
- Queue length and wait time at 8am weekday, 1pm, and a weekend morning.
- Ordering channels (counter, app, delivery), loyalty program.
- Space: seats used, laptop share, Wi-Fi, outlets, restroom.
- Quality notes (espresso taste, milk texture) and service language (English/Spanish).

## Output
- A table sorted by distance, a price-ladder comparison against our planned menu, and a short "gaps we can own" list.
- Date every observation; prices and ratings go stale quickly.
