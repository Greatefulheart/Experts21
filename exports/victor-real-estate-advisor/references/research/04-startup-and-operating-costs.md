# 4. Startup and Operating Costs

## A. Startup budget

### National benchmarks
| Source | Typical range |
|---|---|
| [Bellwether Coffee (2026)](https://bellwethercoffee.com/blog/coffee-shop-startup-costs) | $80K–$300K; midpoint ~$200K |
| [VantaInsights (2026)](https://vantainsights.com/insights/coffee-shop-startup-costs) | Kiosk $25K–$75K; seated café $80K–$300K; drive-thru $80K–$200K |
| [DoorDash Merchants (Nov 2026)](https://merchants.doordash.com/en-us/blog/how-much-does-it-cost-to-open-a-coffee-shop) | ~$75K to $320K+ |
| [Delta Capital Group (2026)](https://deltacapitalgroup.com/how-much-does-it-cost-to-open-a-coffee-shop/) | $80K–$300K, some up to $400K |

### Miami café budget (Estimate: ~1,000–1,400 sq ft, 25–40 seats)
Miami sits at the upper middle of the national ranges because of rents, contractor costs and hurricane-rated building requirements.

| Category | Low | Mid | High | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Buildout / leasehold improvements | $30K | $80K | $175K | A former café costs $15K–$40K; converting retail space $40K–$80K; a bare shell $80K–$150K+ (Bellwether) |
| Espresso and brew equipment | $20K | $40K | $70K | 2-group espresso machine $10K–$35K; grinders $3K–$8K; water filtration $1.5K–$6K |
| Refrigeration, ice machine, smallwares | $6K | $15K | $25K | |
| Furniture, fixtures, signage | $8K | $20K | $35K | |
| POS, Wi-Fi, cameras, music | $2K | $5K | $10K | |
| Licenses, permits, legal, accounting, architect | $5K | $12K | $25K | Architect or designer fees can run $2K–$15K |
| Initial inventory and packaging | $5K | $8K | $15K | |
| Pre-opening marketing and brand | $4K | $10K | $20K | |
| Security deposit plus first and last month's rent | $15K | $25K | $45K | |
| Pre-opening payroll and training | $3K | $6K | $10K | |
| Contingency (10–15%) | $10K | $25K | $45K | Buildout overruns typically run 10–30% (Bellwether) |
| Working capital (3–6 months) | $40K | $70K | $110K | Owners often underestimate this the most |
| **Total** | **~$150K** | **~$315K** | **~$585K** | |

**Biggest savings:** take over a space that was already a café; negotiate free rent months and a tenant-improvement allowance from the landlord; buy certified pre-owned equipment; start with a short menu and no full kitchen.

**Optional roaster:** an electric ventless roaster costs about $15K–$20K. Bellwether, which sells roasters, says it can cut coffee cost by 30–50% and pay for itself in 6–12 months. Treat that claim with caution because of the source.

*Assumption:* most Miami retail leases are NNN, meaning the tenant also pays a share of building costs (CAM), property tax and building insurance. Add **$10–$20/sq ft** for these until real quotes come in. A 1,200 sq ft space at $45 base rent plus $15 NNN costs about **$6,000 a month**.

## C. Monthly operating costs

### Benchmarks (share of revenue)
| Line | Benchmark | Source |
|---|---|---|
| COGS (cost of goods: coffee, milk, food, packaging) | 25–35% overall; 15–20% on espresso drinks alone | [VantaInsights](https://vantainsights.com/insights/coffee-shop-profit-margins), [BZZ](https://www.bzz-app.com/industry-cost-benchmarks/coffee-shop) |
| Labor | 28–35% for an independent shop | [HGA](https://hospitalitygrowthadvisors.com/insights/restaurant-labor-cost-percentage), BZZ |
| Card processing | 2.5–3.5% of sales | Bellwether, VantaInsights |
| Music licensing (ASCAP, BMI, SESAC) | $500–$1,500 a year | VantaInsights |
| **Net margin** | **2.5–7%** on average | VantaInsights |

### Example monthly P&L (Estimate, built on the assumptions above)
| Line | Monthly revenue $45K | Monthly revenue $65K |
|---|---|---|
| Daily transactions at a $7.50 average ticket | ~200 | ~290 |
| COGS (30%) | $13,500 | $19,500 |
| Labor including payroll taxes (see 06-staffing) | $19,000 | $21,500 |
| Occupancy (rent plus NNN) | $6,000 | $6,000 |
| Card fees (3%) | $1,350 | $1,950 |
| Utilities (electricity for AC and espresso machine, water, internet) | $1,300 | $1,500 |
| Insurance (Estimate, see 08-risks) | $600 | $600 |
| Marketing, software, repairs, accounting, licensing, supplies | $2,500 | $3,000 |
| **Operating profit before debt and owner pay** | **~$750 (1.7%)** | **~$10,950 (16.8%)** |

**Main point:** fixed costs (rent, a minimum staffing level and a manager) are large. Below about $45K a month in revenue, the shop roughly breaks even or loses money. Each extra $1K of revenue above that adds about $0.5K of profit. Getting volume through the door matters more than anything else. *Estimate:* break-even is about **190–210 transactions a day**.

Revenue reference: BizBuySell data (2021) showed coffee shops averaging about **$730K a year in revenue with roughly a 9% net margin**. Most shops take **12–24 months** to become profitable. ([Delta Capital Group](https://deltacapitalgroup.com/how-much-does-it-cost-to-open-a-coffee-shop/))

## D. Sales tax (built into prices)
- Florida's state rate of 6% plus Miami-Dade's 1% surtax gives **7%** on prepared food and drinks. ([Florida DOR](https://floridarevenue.com/taxes/taxesfees/Pages/sales_tax.aspx), [Mosey](https://www.moseyfoodfinder.com/us/business/florida-restaurant-sales-tax-rate-and-county-surtax/))
- Some locations carry extra local food-and-beverage taxes. Hotel-based outlets pay a 2% tourist surtax, and Miami Beach charges a resort tax. ([Miami-Dade](https://www.miamidade.gov/global/service.page?Mduid_service=ser1499797928395868)) *To verify for each site.*
