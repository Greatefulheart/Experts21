## Customer segments
These segments are our own working hypotheses, drawn from the research above. Field observation should test them.

| Segment | Where they are | What they want | Visit pattern | Typical ticket (Estimate) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Young professionals** (25–39) | Brickell, Downtown, Edgewater, Wynwood | Fast, high-quality espresso drinks, mobile ordering, oat and other alternative milks, iced drinks | Weekday mornings, 7–10am; daily habit | $6–9 |
| **Remote and hybrid workers, students** | Coral Gables (UM), Brickell, Wynwood, Little River | Wi-Fi, outlets, air conditioning, the ability to stay a long time; one drink plus food | Late morning to afternoon; long stays | $8–14 |
| **Bilingual locals who drink Cuban coffee** | Everywhere, especially Little Havana, Westchester, Hialeah, Coral Gables | Cafecito, colada, cortadito, café con leche at a familiar price; Spanish-language service | Several short visits a day; ventanita style | $3–5 |
| **Tourists and seasonal residents** | Miami Beach, Wynwood, Brickell, Downtown | A "Miami" experience: a photogenic space, a local story, a signature drink, retail bags to take home | Peaks Nov–Apr, weekends, during events | $8–15, plus retail |
| **Health- and wellness-focused customers** | Coral Gables, Miami Beach, Brickell | Functional and adaptogenic drinks (for example, GROU sells a "Chagaccino"), matcha, low-sugar options | Morning or after a workout | $7–10 |
| **Offices and catering (B2B)** | Downtown/Brickell office towers | Coffee boxes, meeting catering, office coffee contracts | Weekdays, planned in advance | $50–300 per order |

## Things specific to Miami
- **Bilingual is the default.** Menus, signs, social media and staff should work in English and Spanish.
- **Customers judge prices against the ventanita.** A ~$3 colada is the reference price. Explain the higher prices with quality you can show (origin, a visible roaster, latte art, the space).
- **Heat:** iced and cold drinks such as cold brew and iced lattes are likely to sell all year. Air conditioning and shade matter as much as the decor.
- **Getting there:** in Brickell and Downtown, people mostly walk. In Coral Gables and the suburbs, people mostly drive, so parking matters.

## What it means for us
- Choose **one main segment plus one secondary segment** for each site. For example, Brickell: young professionals (main) and offices/catering (secondary).
- Price in tiers: a low-cost Cuban espresso drink to bring people in, specialty drinks in the middle, and premium pour-overs, signature drinks and retail at the top.
