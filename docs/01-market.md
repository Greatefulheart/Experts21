# 1. Market

## Key facts

### National
- **US coffee shops overall:** IBISWorld puts industry revenue at about **$74.3B in 2025**. It forecasts only about **1.3% a year** growth to 2030. ([MMCG summary](https://www.mmcginvest.com/post/u-s-coffee-shop-industry-market-analysis-navigating-maturity-margin-pressure-and-the-mandate-fo))
- **Specialty is growing much faster than coffee shops overall.** Grand View Research projects US specialty coffee growing about **9.5% a year from 2025 to 2030**, to roughly $81.8B (same source). SNS Insider values US specialty coffee *shops* at **$45.4B in 2025**, growing about 7.2% a year to 2035. ([SNS Insider](https://www.snsinsider.com/reports/specialty-coffee-shops-market-11720))
- **Younger adults drive the category.** **69% of 25–39-year-olds** drank specialty coffee in the past week, more than any other age group. ([NCA / SCA Specialty Coffee Report](https://www.ncausa.org/newsroom/Specialty-Coffee-Consumption-Remains-at-Record-High))
- **Mobile ordering is now normal.** **36%** of people who bought coffee away from home in the past week ordered through a mobile app, a record high (NACS, Sept 2025, cited by SNS Insider).
- **Takeaway and drive-thru** is the fastest-growing channel. Non-coffee drinks and food is the fastest-growing product segment (SNS Insider).

### Miami
- **Demand:** as reported by Regulr, WalletHub ranked Miami **4th nationally for coffee lovers in 2025** and **12th for coffee shops per capita**. That points to high demand with only moderate supply. ([Regulr](https://regulr.ai/for/coffee-shops/miami); not checked against WalletHub's original ranking)
- **Population:** Miami-Dade has about **2.80M residents (2025)**, **70.6% Hispanic or Latino**. ([Miami-Dade Matters](https://www.miamidadematters.org/demographicdata))
- **Visitors:** about **20.1M overnight visitors a year** to Greater Miami and Miami Beach (GMCVB, 2024 figure). ([GMCVB](https://www.miamiandbeaches.com/getmedia/83b6188f-21c0-40bd-b32d-f8241c12d8f7/GMCVB_State_of_the_Industry_May_29_2025.pdf))
- **Retail space is tight.** Miami-Dade retail vacancy was **2.7%** in Q4 2023, with asking rent around **$44.66/sq ft**. ([MMG Equity Partners](https://mmgequitypartners.com/south-florida-retail-summary/))
- **Cuban coffee culture:** the ventanita (walk-up window), cafecito, colada and cortadito are everyday rituals. Miami already drinks a lot of espresso, served sweet and strong. ([Perfect Daily Grind](https://perfectdailygrind.com/2020/05/a-guide-to-miami-coffee-culture/))

### Coffee commodity backdrop
- Arabica futures hit an **all-time high of $4.41/lb in Feb 2025**. ([Perfect Daily Grind](https://perfectdailygrind.com/2026/02/will-2026-be-different-coffee-industry-challenges/))
- Prices were **about $2.72/lb on Nov 20, 2026**, down about 32% year on year. The USDA forecasts a record Brazilian crop of **72.5M bags for 2026/27**. ([Trading Economics](https://tradingeconomics.com/commodity/coffee))
- The US put a tariff of up to 50% on Brazilian goods in 2025, then removed it for green coffee in Nov 2025. ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/global-coffee-prices-plunge-after-trump-removes-tariffs-brazil-2025-11-21/))

## What it means for us
- **There is room for a specialty shop, but it has to stand out.** Overall coffee-shop growth is flat. The growth is in premium, experience-led and convenience-led formats.
- **Miami customers already drink espresso.** The job is to move them up from a $3 cafecito to a $5–7 specialty drink, not to teach them to drink coffee.
- **Expect seasons.** Tourist traffic peaks from November to April. Summer brings heat, rain and hurricane season.
- **Bean costs are easing heading into 2027.** That makes it a better moment to lock in roaster contracts than 2025 was.
