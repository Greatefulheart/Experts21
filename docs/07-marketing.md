# 7. Marketing

These recommendations are built on the research in files 01–03. The figures with sources are noted; everything else is our recommendation.

## Positioning options (choose one)
1. **Cuban × specialty:** high-quality, traceable beans prepared as cafecito, colada and cortadito, alongside a specialty espresso and pour-over menu. This links Miami's everyday habit with the premium market, with a walk-up ventanita window plus a café inside.
2. **Neighborhood "third place" or cowork café:** like GROU in Coral Gables. Earn from long stays with food, memberships and events.
3. **Grab-and-go for commuters:** a small space in Brickell or Downtown, built around mobile pre-ordering and speed.

## Channels
| Channel | Why it matters | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **Google Business Profile** | Guides and directories rank coffee shops by Google rating and review count; La Colada Gourmet has **3,823** reviews | Set it up before opening; ask for reviews; reply in English and Spanish |
| **Instagram and TikTok** | Miami's cafés are image-driven: art, design, signature drinks | 3–5 posts a week; reels of the drink being made; work with local creators |
| **Mobile ordering and loyalty app** | **36%** of people who bought coffee away from home used an app ([NACS via SNS Insider](https://www.snsinsider.com/reports/specialty-coffee-shops-market-11720)) | Use a POS with online ordering, loyalty and gift cards (for example, Square or Toast) |
| **Delivery marketplaces** | Reach nearby customers before the brand is known ([DoorDash](https://merchants.doordash.com/en-us/blog/how-much-does-it-cost-to-open-a-coffee-shop)) | Use them for the first 3–6 months, then review the commission cost |
| **Curated guides and directories** | BrewAtlas, The Infatuation, Miami New Times, Reddit r/Miami all drive discovery | Pitch to local food writers; invite them to the soft opening |
| **B2B and offices** | Brickell and Downtown towers | Coffee boxes, catering menus, office coffee contracts |
| **Wholesale and retail bags** | Tourists take beans home; brings revenue in slow months | Sell bagged beans (our own or a partner roaster's) and merchandise |

## Calendar hooks (Miami)
- **Nov–Apr peak season:** snowbirds and tourists arrive. Plan the opening for October or November if possible.
- **Art Basel Miami Beach (early December) and Miami Art Week:** pop-ups and collaborations with Wynwood galleries.
- **Miami Music Week / Ultra (March), Miami Open, F1 Miami Grand Prix (spring):** event-driven traffic and possible catering.
- **National Coffee Day (Sept 29):** promotion in the slow season.
- **Summer slowdown (June–Sept):** loyalty pushes, iced-drink launches, offers for residents, events.
- *To verify:* confirm the dates for each year.

## Launch plan (Estimate, 8 weeks)
| When | What |
|---|---|
| T-8 weeks | Brand, bilingual menu, Google profile, Instagram account with a buildout "coming soon" series |
| T-4 weeks | Build an email/SMS list (free drink at opening); meet neighbors and building managers |
| T-2 weeks | Soft opening for neighbors, creators and press; collect reviews |
| Opening week | Grand opening event; loyalty sign-up offer; tasting flights |
| +1–3 months | Weekly content; first B2B accounts; adjust the menu using POS data |

**Budget:** $4K–$20K before opening, then **1–3% of revenue** each month. *(Estimate)*

## Metrics to track
- Daily transactions, average ticket and attach rate (food with a drink)
- Share of revenue from loyalty members and repeat visit rate
- Google rating and review count, social media reach, app order share
- Cost to acquire each new loyalty member
