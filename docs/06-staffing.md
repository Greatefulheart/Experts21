## Hiring compliance
- **E-Verify:** private employers with **25 or more employees** must use E-Verify for new hires (Florida Statutes 448.095, in force since July 1, 2023). Smaller employers may use Form I-9 instead. Note that 25 includes part-time staff. ([FDOR FAQ](https://floridarevenue.com/taxes/Documents/eVerifyFAQs.pdf), [Shutts](https://www.shutts.com/business-and-legal-insights/florida-employers-subject-to-e-verify-law-beginning-july-1-2023))
- **Food manager certification:** at least one certified manager is needed. If **3 or more food employees** work at the same time, a certified manager must be **present at all times**. ([Florida DOH](https://www.floridahealth.gov/licensing-regulations/regulated-facilities/food-safety-and-sanitation/food-manager-certification/), [DBPR](https://www2.myfloridalicense.com/hotels-restaurants/food-lodging/food-manager/))
- **Food employee training** for all staff who handle food (see 05-permits).
- **Workers' compensation:** Florida requires it for non-construction employers with **4 or more employees** (*To verify*).

## Labor model (Estimate)
- Open hours: 7am–6pm, 7 days a week, which is 77 hours a week.
- Coverage: 2 baristas at all times, a 3rd during the 7–11am rush and on weekends. That is about **190 barista hours a week**.
- At a blended $16.50/hr, that is about $3,135 a week, or **about $13,600 a month**.
- Add the manager at about $5,400 a month, then payroll taxes and workers' comp at about 10%. **Total labor is about $19,000–$21,500 a month.**
- Labor stays at the 28–35% benchmark only if revenue is **$60K or more a month**. Below that, cut hours (for example, close at 3pm) or run with a leaner team.

## Turnover and retention
- Barista turnover is high across the industry. Expect to replace much of the team every year. *To verify:* look for a Miami-specific turnover rate.
- Ways to keep staff: tip pooling plus a living-wage base; SCA barista training and a career path; predictable schedules; free drinks and meals; small profit-share or bonus pools tied to reviews or sales.
