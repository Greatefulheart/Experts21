# Miami Specialty Coffee Shop — Research Pack

Background research on opening an independent **specialty coffee shop in Miami, Florida**. It is written as shared context for the Experts21 team of expert agents. Each agent should read this index first, then the files for its area.

- **Research date:** November 2026
- **Scope:** City of Miami and the wider Miami-Dade County. The county has more than 30 municipalities, such as Miami Beach and Coral Gables, and each one runs its own permits.
- **Status:** desk research from public sources. Nothing here has been checked with a broker, attorney, accountant or regulator yet.

## Headline findings

1. **Demand is strong.** As reported by a secondary source, WalletHub ranked Miami 4th in the US for coffee lovers in 2025. Miami also has a deep Cuban coffee culture and about 20 million overnight visitors a year.
2. **Rivals set the price floor at about $3.** Specialty roasters (Panther, Vice City Bean, Pasión del Cielo and others) are already in Wynwood, Brickell, Edgewater and Coral Gables. Cuban ventanitas sell a cafecito or colada for about $3, and customers judge everything else against that price.
3. **A seated café costs about $150K–$350K to open in Miami (our mid-case estimate is ~$315K).** Taking over a space that was already a café is the biggest single saving. Average retail asking rent in Miami is about $45/sq ft per year.
4. **Labor is the cost that decides profit.** Florida's minimum wage reached $15.00/hr on Sept 30, 2026. Plan on labor taking 28–35% of revenue.
5. **Margins are thin.** Typical net margin is 2.5–7%. The biggest risks are running out of cash, the terms of the lease, the cost of labor, hurricanes and insurance, the summer slump, and swings in green coffee prices.

## Conventions used in these files

- **Facts** come with a source link and, where known, a date.
- **Estimate:** marks a planning number we built from several sources. Check these before making a decision.
- **Assumption:** marks a value we chose so a model would work. Replace these with real quotes.
- **To verify** lists open questions that a person or an agent with live access should settle.

## Recommended next steps for the agent team

1. Pick two or three candidate neighborhoods and get real listing rents and CAM/NNN charges for them.
2. Write a concept brief: format (kiosk, café or roastery-café), menu, price ladder and seat count.
3. Replace the assumptions in `04-startup-and-operating-costs.md` with real quotes, then build a 36-month financial model.
4. Confirm the permit path with the municipality that has jurisdiction over the chosen site.
